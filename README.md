<h1 align="center">🧠 Mental Vital Index (MVI)</h1>
<h3 align="center">Voice-Based Mental Health Monitoring</h3>

<p align="center">
A web-based mental health monitoring system that helps users track, understand, and improve their mental well-being using voice and text analysis.
</p>

<h2>🌟 Overview</h2>

Mental health is deeply connected to how we speak, write, and express emotions.
MVI analyzes short voice recordings and text inputs to identify emotional patterns and generate a Mental Vitality Score (MVI Score) that reflects overall mental wellness.

The goal is early awareness, self-reflection, and continuous improvement, not diagnosis.

✨ Key Features

1.🎙️ Voice Analysis

2.Analyze 30–60 second voice recordings

3.Detect speech patterns, tone, and emotional signals

4.Helps identify stress, fatigue, or emotional imbalance

<h2>📝 Text Analysis</h2>

1.Optional text-based input for mental state assessment

2.Uses natural language processing to understand sentiment and stress levels

<h2>📊 MVI Score</h2>

1.A comprehensive mental vitality score (0–100)

2.Combines multiple analysis signals into a single metric

3.Easy to understand and track over time

<h2>📈 Trend Tracking</h2>

1.Monitor changes in mental well-being over time

2.Identify patterns and measure improvement

3.Useful for long-term self-awareness

<h2>🎯 Personalized Recommendations</h2>

1.Tailored suggestions based on analysis results

2.Includes breathing exercises, sleep hygiene tips, and lifestyle guidance

<h2>🧑‍⚕️ Provider Dashboard</h2>

1.Designed for healthcare professionals

2.Monitor multiple users and track intervention effectiveness

3.Supports data-driven mental health support

<h2>⚙️ How It Works</h2>

1.Record or Write
Share how you’re feeling using voice or text (30–60 seconds)

2.Analysis
System analyzes speech and content patterns

3.MVI Score
Receive a comprehensive mental vitality score

4.Recommendations
Get personalized action steps for improvement

5.Track Progress
Monitor trends and celebrate improvements over time
